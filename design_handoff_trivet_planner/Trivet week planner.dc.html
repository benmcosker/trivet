Print rules
Black on white, both pages. Tick boxes on the list, none on the week. An unplanned day prints as an em dash rather than being omitted, so the week reads as seven days with two open rather than as a five-day week. No photos: they are the most expensive thing on the page and identify nothing you don't already know by the time you are holding it.
