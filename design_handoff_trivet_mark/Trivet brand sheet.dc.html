Trivet

The mark, its lockups, the app icon, the warm dark scheme and three contrast corrections to the light one. The type and layout system shipped with the identity pass and is not restated here.
01

The mark

viewBox 0 0 100 100 · centre 50,50
ring r=30 · stroke 8
legs at 90°, 210°, 330° · round caps
legs run r=30 → r=45, radially
hub r=10, filled · no stroke

A ring standing on three legs, holding something hot. The tri of the name sits outside the ring rather than inside it, which is both what a trivet looks like from above and what keeps the mark from reading as a three-pointed star — nothing converges at the centre, because the centre is occupied.

The clay hub is the one piece carried over from the placeholder. It is the dish, not an accent: the mark is green and clay in that order, and the two never swap.

Clear space

Half the ring's diameter on every side, measured from the leg tips rather than the ring. Nothing crosses it, and the mark is never set over a photograph.

Minimum size

16px. The geometry never simplifies — the strokes thicken and the legs shorten slightly, because two legs is a different object.

Don't

Rotate it, outline the hub, recolour the hub green, or add a fourth leg. It stands on three, feet down.

The wordmark is Newsreader 400, set plainly. One word has nothing to split, so there is no roman/italic device as there was in “Meal Magic”. In the horizontal lockup the mark's ring — not its leg tips — centres on the wordmark's x-height band.

03

App icon

Three variants, because three contexts. A browser tab picks its own background, so the favicon carries a tile. iOS applies its own mask and never needs a radius baked in. A README sits on white already.

Green tile, paper mark
tile #2f6f4e · rx 7/32
mark #f7f4ed · hub #efb08b
mark inset to r=28

icon.svg — the default. The hub lifts to a lighter clay here: #a44a24 on green is 1.2:1 and disappears at 16px, where #efb08b holds at 3.2.

Paper tile, green mark
tile #f7f4ed · no radius baked
mark #2f6f4e · hub #a44a24
export 1024×1024 flat

apple-icon.png — the home-screen icon. Paper reads as an app rather than a folder, and iOS rounds the corners itself.

Transparent, ink mark
no tile · currentColor
full-bleed to r=45

README, docs, printed pages, the Claude Code brief. No tile means the legs run to the edge of the box, so the mark reads larger at the same dimensions.
